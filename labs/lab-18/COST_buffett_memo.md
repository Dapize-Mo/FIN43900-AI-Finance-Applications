## Verdict: **OWN THE BUSINESS**

Charlie and I have spent our entire lives looking for businesses with wide economic moats, predictable earning power, and managers who allocate capital like owners.
COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP /NEW (COST) is the textbook definition of a wonderful business that meets all three of our quantitative hurdles without exception.

## 1. Consistent High Return on Capital

Over the five fiscal years from 2022-08-28 to 2026-08-30, return on year-end equity was 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, 25.8%. Every single year comfortably exceeded our 15.0% hurdle rate. In 2022-08-28, the company earned 5,844 million on 20,642 million of equity (28.3%), and by 2026-08-30, net income grew to 9,226 million on 35,803 million of equity (25.8%). That tells me the company is earning extraordinary returns on the money retained in the business without taking on dangerous leverage.

## 2. Abundant and Growing Owner Earnings

Accounting earnings can deceive, but owner earnings tell the unvarnished economic truth. When we take operating cash flow and subtract the capital expenditures required to maintain competitive standing, COST's owner earnings were positive every single year:

- **FY 2022-08-28:** Operating cash flow of 7,392 million less capital spending of 3,891 million produced owner earnings of **3,501 million**.
- **FY 2023-09-03:** Operating cash flow of 11,068 million less capital spending of 4,323 million produced owner earnings of **6,745 million**.
- **FY 2024-09-01:** Operating cash flow of 11,339 million less capital spending of 4,710 million produced owner earnings of **6,629 million**.
- **FY 2025-08-31:** Operating cash flow of 13,335 million less capital spending of 5,498 million produced owner earnings of **7,837 million**.
- **FY 2026-08-30:** Operating cash flow of 15,817 million less capital spending of 6,435 million produced owner earnings of **9,382 million**.

Crucially, owner earnings expanded from 3,501 million in 2022-08-28 to 9,382 million in 2026-08-30, compounding strongly alongside revenue growth from 226,954 million to 303,154 million. That is the hallmark of a franchise that doesn't just eat capital, but showers cash on its owners.

## 3. Business Moat & Fiduciary Conclusion

The business model demonstrates pricing power, operational discipline, and an entrenched consumer habit that competitors cannot dislodge. Because our three hurdles all passed (ROE >= 15% every year: pass; owner earnings positive: pass; owner earnings grew: pass), this is an enterprise I would happily own for fifty years if the market closed tomorrow.
