# Warren Buffett Investment Memo: PEPSICO INC (PEP)
**From:** Warren E. Buffett, Chairman & CEO, Berkshire Hathaway
**To:** Charlie Munger & Investment Committee
**Topic:** 5-Year Business Quality Assessment for PEPSICO INC (PEP)
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K filings (2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27)
**Scope:** Business fundamentals evaluation only (no stock price or market valuation inputs)

---

### Verdict: **OWN THE BUSINESS**

Charlie and I have spent our entire lives looking for businesses with wide economic moats, predictable earning power, and managers who allocate capital like owners. 
PEPSICO INC (PEP) is the textbook definition of a wonderful business that meets all three of our quantitative hurdles without exception.

### 1. Consistent High Return on Capital
Over the five fiscal years from 2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27, return on year-end equity was 47.5%, 52.0%, 49.0%, 53.1%, 40.4%. Every single year comfortably exceeded our 15.0% hurdle rate. In 2021-12-25, the company earned 7,618 million on 16,043 million of equity (47.5%), and by 2025-12-27, net income grew to 8,240 million on 20,406 million of equity (40.4%). That tells me the company is earning extraordinary returns on the money retained in the business without taking on dangerous leverage.

### 2. Abundant and Growing Owner Earnings
Accounting earnings can deceive, but owner earnings tell the unvarnished economic truth. When we take operating cash flow and subtract the capital expenditures required to maintain competitive standing, PEP's owner earnings were positive every single year:
- **FY 2021-12-25:** Operating cash flow of 11,616 million less capital spending of 4,625 million produced owner earnings of **6,991 million**.
- **FY 2022-12-31:** Operating cash flow of 10,811 million less capital spending of 5,207 million produced owner earnings of **5,604 million**.
- **FY 2023-12-30:** Operating cash flow of 13,442 million less capital spending of 5,518 million produced owner earnings of **7,924 million**.
- **FY 2024-12-28:** Operating cash flow of 12,507 million less capital spending of 5,318 million produced owner earnings of **7,189 million**.
- **FY 2025-12-27:** Operating cash flow of 12,087 million less capital spending of 4,415 million produced owner earnings of **7,672 million**.

Crucially, owner earnings expanded from 6,991 million in 2021-12-25 to 7,672 million in 2025-12-27, compounding strongly alongside revenue growth from 79,474 million to 93,925 million. That is the hallmark of a franchise that doesn't just eat capital, but showers cash on its owners.

### 3. Business Moat & Fiduciary Conclusion
The business model demonstrates pricing power, operational discipline, and an entrenched consumer habit that competitors cannot dislodge. Because our three hurdles all passed (ROE >= 15% every year: pass; owner earnings positive: pass; owner earnings grew: pass), this is an enterprise I would happily own for fifty years if the market closed tomorrow.
