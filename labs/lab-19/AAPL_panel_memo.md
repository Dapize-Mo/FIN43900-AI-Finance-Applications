## 2. Warren Buffett Memo
# Warren Buffett Investment Memo: Apple Inc. (AAPL)
**From:** Warren E. Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway
**To:** Charlie Munger & Investment Committee
**Topic:** 5-Year Business Quality Assessment for Apple Inc. (AAPL)
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K filings (2022-09-24 to 2026-09-26)
**Scope:** Business fundamentals only (no stock price or market valuation inputs)

---

## Verdict: **OWN THE BUSINESS**

Charlie and I have spent our entire lives seeking businesses with wide economic moats, predictable earning power, and managers who allocate capital like owners. 
Apple Inc. (AAPL) is the textbook definition of an exceptional business that satisfies all three of our quantitative hurdles without exception.

### 1. Consistent High Return on Capital

Over the five fiscal years from 2022-09-24 to 2026-09-26, return on year-end equity was 197.0%, 156.1%, 164.6%, 151.9%, 117.9%. Every year comfortably exceeded our 15.0% hurdle rate. In 2022-09-24, the company earned 99,803 million on 50,672 million of equity (197.0%), and by 2026-09-26, net income grew to 132,175 million on 112,093 million of equity (117.9%). That proves extraordinary capital productivity without dangerous balance sheet leverage.

### 2. Abundant and Growing Owner Earnings

Accounting earnings can deceive, but owner earnings reveal economic reality. Subtracting capital expenditures from operating cash flow demonstrates positive owner cash generation every single year:

- **FY 2022-09-24:** Operating cash flow of 122,151 million less capital spending of 10,708 million produced owner earnings of **111,443 million**.
- **FY 2023-09-30:** Operating cash flow of 110,543 million less capital spending of 10,959 million produced owner earnings of **99,584 million**.
- **FY 2024-09-28:** Operating cash flow of 118,254 million less capital spending of 9,447 million produced owner earnings of **108,807 million**.
- **FY 2025-09-27:** Operating cash flow of 111,482 million less capital spending of 12,715 million produced owner earnings of **98,767 million**.
- **FY 2026-09-26:** Operating cash flow of 145,097 million less capital spending of 9,041 million produced owner earnings of **136,056 million**.

Owner earnings expanded from 111,443 million in 2022-09-24 to 136,056 million in 2026-09-26, compounding alongside revenue growth from 394,328 million to 478,834 million. This franchise showers cash on its owners.

### 3. Moat & Conclusion

The business demonstrates pricing power, operational discipline, and an entrenched consumer habit. Because our three hurdles all passed (ROE >= 15.0%: pass; owner earnings positive: pass; owner earnings grew: pass), this is an enterprise I would happily own for fifty years.

## 3. Benjamin Graham Memo
# Benjamin Graham Investment Memo: Apple Inc. (AAPL)
**From:** Benjamin Graham, Security Analyst
**To:** Investment Committee & Serious Investors
**Topic:** Balance Sheet Margin of Safety & Earnings Stability Review
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K reported facts (2022-09-24 to 2026-09-26)
**Scope:** Defensive investor appraisal based on primary balance sheet and income statements

---

## Verdict: **REJECT / SPECULATIVE**

### 1. Working Capital Margin of Safety (Current Ratio)

In FY 2026-09-26, current assets stood at 173,857 million against current liabilities of 169,915 million, yielding a current ratio of **1.02**. This **fails** our classical defensive investor standard of 2.0. An enterprise operating below this liquidity threshold lacks the protective cushion required to insulate conservative capital against severe economic contractions.

### 2. Earnings Stability & Ten-Year Trend

Over the five reported fiscal years, net income was 99,803 million, 96,995 million, 93,736 million, 112,010 million, 132,175 million. 
Earnings were positive in all five fiscal years, fulfilling our stability requirement. 
Furthermore, net income expanded from 99,803 million in 2022-09-24 to 132,175 million in 2026-09-26, demonstrating legitimate earnings growth across the cycle.

### 3. Quantitative Disposition & Threshold Design Choice

While earnings stability is proven, the working capital ratio of 1.02 violates our classical 2.0 defensive standard. We cannot classify an enterprise with inadequate working capital margin as a defensive holding, regardless of past growth.

**Threshold Decision:** Graham threshold kept at 2.0 (classical defensive investor margin of safety); lowered to 1.0 for capital-efficient negative-working-capital retailers like Costco.

## 4. Michael Burry Memo
# Michael Burry Forensic Memo: Apple Inc. (AAPL)
**From:** Michael Burry, Scion Asset Management
**To:** Scion Investment Committee & Fund Partners
**Topic:** 10-K Forensic Accounting, Cash Conversion & Leverage Audit
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K primary audited statements (2022-09-24 to 2026-09-26)
**Scope:** Forensic divergence between reported net income and cash flow, plus debt cushion

---

## Verdict: **FAIL FORENSIC SCREEN**

### 1. Earnings Quality: Operating Cash Flow vs. Net Income

Reported accounting income is a narrative crafted by management. Cash flow is the cold, hard reality. We compare Operating Cash Flow (OCF) directly to Net Income (NI) for every period:

- **FY 2022-09-24:** Operating Cash Flow of 122,151 million vs. Net Income of 99,803 million.
- **FY 2023-09-30:** Operating Cash Flow of 110,543 million vs. Net Income of 96,995 million.
- **FY 2024-09-28:** Operating Cash Flow of 118,254 million vs. Net Income of 93,736 million.
- **FY 2025-09-27:** Operating Cash Flow of 111,482 million vs. Net Income of 112,010 million.
- **FY 2026-09-26:** Operating Cash Flow of 145,097 million vs. Net Income of 132,175 million.

Operating cash flow lagged reported net income in one or more years. When cash generation trails reported accounting earnings, accruals are inflating profits, indicating earnings quality deterioration.

### 2. Balance Sheet Cushion: Total Liabilities vs. Equity

In FY 2026-09-26, total liabilities stood at 292,455 million against stockholders' equity of 112,093 million, resulting in a liabilities-to-equity ratio of **2.61**.

This **fails** our forensic ceiling of 2.0. Total liabilities exceed twice the equity base, creating elevated financial risk and reducing margin of error.

### 3. Contrarian Synthesis

One or more forensic hurdles failed. Consensus Wall Street ignores these structural discrepancies, but our quantitative audit identifies material risk.
