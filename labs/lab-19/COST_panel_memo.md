## Verdict: **OWN THE BUSINESS**

Charlie and I have spent our entire lives seeking businesses with wide economic moats, predictable earning power, and managers who allocate capital like owners. 
COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP /NEW (COST) is the textbook definition of an exceptional business that satisfies all three of our quantitative hurdles without exception.

### 1. Consistent High Return on Capital

Over the five fiscal years from 2022-08-28 to 2026-08-30, return on year-end equity was 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, 25.8%. Every year comfortably exceeded our 15.0% hurdle rate. In 2022-08-28, the company earned 5,844 million on 20,642 million of equity (28.3%), and by 2026-08-30, net income grew to 9,226 million on 35,803 million of equity (25.8%). That proves extraordinary capital productivity without dangerous balance sheet leverage.

### 2. Abundant and Growing Owner Earnings

Accounting earnings can deceive, but owner earnings reveal economic reality. Subtracting capital expenditures from operating cash flow demonstrates positive owner cash generation every single year:

- **FY 2022-08-28:** Operating cash flow of 7,392 million less capital spending of 3,891 million produced owner earnings of **3,501 million**.
- **FY 2023-09-03:** Operating cash flow of 11,068 million less capital spending of 4,323 million produced owner earnings of **6,745 million**.
- **FY 2024-09-01:** Operating cash flow of 11,339 million less capital spending of 4,710 million produced owner earnings of **6,629 million**.
- **FY 2025-08-31:** Operating cash flow of 13,335 million less capital spending of 5,498 million produced owner earnings of **7,837 million**.
- **FY 2026-08-30:** Operating cash flow of 15,817 million less capital spending of 6,435 million produced owner earnings of **9,382 million**.

Owner earnings expanded from 3,501 million in 2022-08-28 to 9,382 million in 2026-08-30, compounding alongside revenue growth from 226,954 million to 303,154 million. This franchise showers cash on its owners.

### 3. Moat & Conclusion

The business demonstrates pricing power, operational discipline, and an entrenched consumer habit. Because our three hurdles all passed (ROE >= 15.0%: pass; owner earnings positive: pass; owner earnings grew: pass), this is an enterprise I would happily own for fifty years.

## 3. Benjamin Graham Memo
# Benjamin Graham Investment Memo: COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP /NEW (COST)
**From:** Benjamin Graham, Security Analyst
**To:** Investment Committee & Serious Investors
**Topic:** Balance Sheet Margin of Safety & Earnings Stability Review
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K reported facts (2022-08-28 to 2026-08-30)
**Scope:** Defensive investor appraisal based on primary balance sheet and income statements

---

## Verdict: **REJECT / SPECULATIVE**

### 1. Working Capital Margin of Safety (Current Ratio)

In FY 2026-08-30, current assets stood at 46,582 million against current liabilities of 43,952 million, yielding a current ratio of **1.06**. This **fails** our classical defensive investor standard of 2.0. An enterprise operating below this liquidity threshold lacks the protective cushion required to insulate conservative capital against severe economic contractions.

### 2. Earnings Stability & Ten-Year Trend

Over the five reported fiscal years, net income was 5,844 million, 6,292 million, 7,367 million, 8,099 million, 9,226 million. 
Earnings were positive in all five fiscal years, fulfilling our stability requirement. 
Furthermore, net income expanded from 5,844 million in 2022-08-28 to 9,226 million in 2026-08-30, demonstrating legitimate earnings growth across the cycle.

### 3. Quantitative Disposition & Threshold Design Choice

While earnings stability is proven, the working capital ratio of 1.06 violates our classical 2.0 defensive standard. We cannot classify an enterprise with inadequate working capital margin as a defensive holding, regardless of past growth.

**Threshold Decision:** Graham threshold kept at 2.0 (classical defensive investor margin of safety); lowered to 1.0 for capital-efficient negative-working-capital retailers like Costco.

- **FY 2024-09-01:** Operating Cash Flow of 11,339 million vs. Net Income of 7,367 million.

- **FY 2026-08-30:** Operating Cash Flow of 15,817 million vs. Net Income of 9,226 million.

Across all five fiscal years from 2022-08-28 to 2026-08-30, operating cash flow equaled or exceeded reported net income. Cash collection is genuine, receivables are turning over, and there is zero evidence of aggressive revenue pulling forward or accrual fabrication. Earnings quality is legitimate.

### 2. Balance Sheet Cushion: Total Liabilities vs. Equity

In FY 2026-08-30, total liabilities stood at 53,242 million against stockholders' equity of 35,803 million, resulting in a liabilities-to-equity ratio of **1.49**.

This comfortably passes our forensic ceiling of 2.0. The enterprise maintains adequate equity cushion relative to total obligations, mitigating structural solvency risk.

### 3. Contrarian Synthesis

Both forensic hurdles passed: cash flow strictly confirms net income and total liabilities remain within 2.0 times equity. There is no forensic basis for a short thesis on earnings quality or solvency.
