## 2. Warren Buffett Memo
# Warren Buffett Investment Memo: PEPSICO INC (PEP)
**From:** Warren E. Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway
**To:** Charlie Munger & Investment Committee
**Topic:** 5-Year Business Quality Assessment for PEPSICO INC (PEP)
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K filings (2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27)
**Scope:** Business fundamentals only (no stock price or market valuation inputs)

---

## Verdict: **OWN THE BUSINESS**

Charlie and I have spent our entire lives seeking businesses with wide economic moats, predictable earning power, and managers who allocate capital like owners. 
PEPSICO INC (PEP) is the textbook definition of an exceptional business that satisfies all three of our quantitative hurdles without exception.

### 1. Consistent High Return on Capital

Over the five fiscal years from 2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27, return on year-end equity was 47.5%, 52.0%, 49.0%, 53.1%, 40.4%. Every year comfortably exceeded our 15.0% hurdle rate. In 2021-12-25, the company earned 7,618 million on 16,043 million of equity (47.5%), and by 2025-12-27, net income grew to 8,240 million on 20,406 million of equity (40.4%). That proves extraordinary capital productivity without dangerous balance sheet leverage.

### 2. Abundant and Growing Owner Earnings

Accounting earnings can deceive, but owner earnings reveal economic reality. Subtracting capital expenditures from operating cash flow demonstrates positive owner cash generation every single year:

- **FY 2021-12-25:** Operating cash flow of 11,616 million less capital spending of 4,625 million produced owner earnings of **6,991 million**.
- **FY 2022-12-31:** Operating cash flow of 10,811 million less capital spending of 5,207 million produced owner earnings of **5,604 million**.
- **FY 2023-12-30:** Operating cash flow of 13,442 million less capital spending of 5,518 million produced owner earnings of **7,924 million**.
- **FY 2024-12-28:** Operating cash flow of 12,507 million less capital spending of 5,318 million produced owner earnings of **7,189 million**.
- **FY 2025-12-27:** Operating cash flow of 12,087 million less capital spending of 4,415 million produced owner earnings of **7,672 million**.

Owner earnings expanded from 6,991 million in 2021-12-25 to 7,672 million in 2025-12-27, compounding alongside revenue growth from 79,474 million to 93,925 million. This franchise showers cash on its owners.

### 3. Moat & Conclusion

The business demonstrates pricing power, operational discipline, and an entrenched consumer habit. Because our three hurdles all passed (ROE >= 15.0%: pass; owner earnings positive: pass; owner earnings grew: pass), this is an enterprise I would happily own for fifty years.

## 3. Benjamin Graham Memo
# Benjamin Graham Investment Memo: PEPSICO INC (PEP)
**From:** Benjamin Graham, Security Analyst
**To:** Investment Committee & Serious Investors
**Topic:** Balance Sheet Margin of Safety & Earnings Stability Review
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K reported facts (2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27)
**Scope:** Defensive investor appraisal based on primary balance sheet and income statements

---

## Verdict: **REJECT / SPECULATIVE**

### 1. Working Capital Margin of Safety (Current Ratio)

In FY 2025-12-27, current assets stood at 27,949 million against current liabilities of 32,764 million, yielding a current ratio of **0.85**. This **fails** our classical defensive investor standard of 2.0. An enterprise operating below this liquidity threshold lacks the protective cushion required to insulate conservative capital against severe economic contractions.

### 2. Earnings Stability & Ten-Year Trend

Over the five reported fiscal years, net income was 7,618 million, 8,910 million, 9,074 million, 9,578 million, 8,240 million. 
Earnings were positive in all five fiscal years, fulfilling our stability requirement. 
Furthermore, net income expanded from 7,618 million in 2021-12-25 to 8,240 million in 2025-12-27, demonstrating legitimate earnings growth across the cycle.

### 3. Quantitative Disposition & Threshold Design Choice

While earnings stability is proven, the working capital ratio of 0.85 violates our classical 2.0 defensive standard. We cannot classify an enterprise with inadequate working capital margin as a defensive holding, regardless of past growth.

**Threshold Decision:** Graham threshold kept at 2.0 (classical defensive investor margin of safety); lowered to 1.0 for capital-efficient negative-working-capital retailers like Costco.

## 4. Michael Burry Memo
# Michael Burry Forensic Memo: PEPSICO INC (PEP)
**From:** Michael Burry, Scion Asset Management
**To:** Scion Investment Committee & Fund Partners
**Topic:** 10-K Forensic Accounting, Cash Conversion & Leverage Audit
**Data Basis:** SEC EDGAR 10-K primary audited statements (2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27)
**Scope:** Forensic divergence between reported net income and cash flow, plus debt cushion

---

## Verdict: **FAIL FORENSIC SCREEN**

### 1. Earnings Quality: Operating Cash Flow vs. Net Income

Reported accounting income is a narrative crafted by management. Cash flow is the cold, hard reality. We compare Operating Cash Flow (OCF) directly to Net Income (NI) for every period:

- **FY 2021-12-25:** Operating Cash Flow of 11,616 million vs. Net Income of 7,618 million.
- **FY 2022-12-31:** Operating Cash Flow of 10,811 million vs. Net Income of 8,910 million.
- **FY 2023-12-30:** Operating Cash Flow of 13,442 million vs. Net Income of 9,074 million.
- **FY 2024-12-28:** Operating Cash Flow of 12,507 million vs. Net Income of 9,578 million.
- **FY 2025-12-27:** Operating Cash Flow of 12,087 million vs. Net Income of 8,240 million.

Across all five fiscal years from 2021-12-25 to 2025-12-27, operating cash flow equaled or exceeded reported net income. Cash collection is genuine, receivables are turning over, and there is zero evidence of aggressive revenue pulling forward or accrual fabrication. Earnings quality is legitimate.

### 2. Balance Sheet Cushion: Total Liabilities vs. Equity

In FY 2025-12-27, total liabilities stood at 86,852 million against stockholders' equity of 20,406 million, resulting in a liabilities-to-equity ratio of **4.26**.

This **fails** our forensic ceiling of 2.0. Total liabilities exceed twice the equity base, creating elevated financial risk and reducing margin of error.

### 3. Contrarian Synthesis

One or more forensic hurdles failed. Consensus Wall Street ignores these structural discrepancies, but our quantitative audit identifies material risk.
