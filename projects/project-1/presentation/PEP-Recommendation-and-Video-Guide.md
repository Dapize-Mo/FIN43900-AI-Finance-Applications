# How the forecast becomes a value

Read this before rehearsing the code or committee video.

| Statement | What it does | How it connects |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Income statement | Sales less costs produces operating profit and net income. | Sales, margins, depreciation and interest flow from drivers/schedules. |

| Balance sheet | Tracks assets, debt, operating liabilities and equity. | Capital spending/depreciation roll assets; retained earnings and payouts roll equity. |

| Cash flow | Shows operating, investing and financing cash movements. | Opening cash plus cash change must equal closing balance-sheet cash. |

## FCFF: cash available to all capital providers

FCFF = EBIT x (1 - tax rate) + depreciation/amortization - capital spending - increase in operating working capital. EBIT is operating profit. NOPAT is EBIT after operating tax. More receivables or inventory generally ties up cash. Borrowing and dividends are financing items, outside FCFF.

## DCF and the enterprise-to-equity bridge

Discount forecast FCFF and continuing value at WACC. Then add qualifying cash/investments and subtract debt and other claims to reach common equity value. Divide by the stated diluted-share proxy. The dates and definitions of every bridge item matter.

Executed bridge (USD billions, rounded): enterprise value 200.94 + cash/STI 10.72 + affiliate book proxy 2.18 - debt 53.21 - NCI 0.17 = common equity 160.45. Dividing by 1,367.89 million proxy shares yields $117.29.
