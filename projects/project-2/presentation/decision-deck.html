Federal Reserve SR 26-2 Pillar: Conceptual Soundness & Developmental Challenge

Visual for Video 1 (≤ 3:00)
The Specific AI Prompt & Generated Artifact

Prompt to LLM: "Generate standard-library Python code to construct a multi-asset covariance matrix from sleeve volatilities and correlation coefficients, and search for the minimum-variance mix."
Raw AI Output: The model produced a functional nested loop, but omitted input validation contracts: it did not enforce symmetry ($\rho_{ij} = \rho_{ji}$) or verify unit diagonals ($\rho_{ii} = 1.0$).

The Mathematical Risk Identified
  • If correlation matrices have asymmetric off-diagonals, the resulting covariance matrix $\mathbf{\Sigma}$ is non-symmetric.
  • A non-symmetric covariance matrix produces an ill-conditioned quadratic surface ($w^T \mathbf{\Sigma} w$).
  • Optimization solvers can converge to invalid local artifacts or report false global minimums.
Human Analyst Verification & Correction
  • Engineered Verification Gate: Wrote `validate_correlation()` in `mandate.py` checking symmetry within $10^{-9}$ tolerance and unit diagonals.
  • Reconciliation Against Lab 14 Known Answers: Reconciled Equal Weight (5.20% ret, 5.21% vol) and Benchmark (6.80% ret, 10.46% vol) down to 4 decimal places.
  • Admitted to Production: Only verified after zero discrepancy was established.

Video 3: Point-in-Time Data Governance (ALFRED Audit)

Dual-Timestamp Architecture & Fiduciary Quarantine of Macroeconomic Series

Visual for Video 3 (≤ 5:00)
Dual Timestamps: $\mathcal{O}(t_{\text{event}}, t_{\text{pub}}, v)$
  • Event Time ($t_{\text{event}}$): The month economic activity occurred (e.g., September 2026).
  • Publication Time ($t_{\text{pub}}$): Date data was compiled and released by BLS (typically 10–14 days lag).
  • Vintage Date ($v$): Snapshot date representing what was historically knowable.
Empirical Finding (Lab 17): On Sept 30, 2026, the Sept CPI print was completely unknowable. Naive backtests that use modern data suffer from severe look-ahead bias!
The 5-Year Seasonal Adjustment Revision Trap
  • BLS Methodology: Every January, the BLS recalculates seasonal factors for the preceding 5 full calendar years.
  • Empirical Audit: Comparing ALFRED vintage 2024-01-15 against today reveals 60 historical months were revised!
  • Fiduciary Quarantine: Raw `CPIAUCSL` is quarantined; models admit data strictly where $t_{\text{pub}} \le t_{\text{decision}}$ or substitute unrevised `CPIAUCNS`.
Point-in-Time Data Card Summary (`CPIAUCSL`)
Provider: BLS via St. Louis Fed ALFRED
Publication Lag: 10–14 days post month-end
