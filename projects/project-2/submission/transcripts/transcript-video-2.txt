[00:25 - 01:00] REPOSITORY TOUR
"The project lives under projects/project-2. The data folder has two files: prices.csv, five years of monthly adjusted prices for four ETF sleeves, BIL, IEF, LQD and VT; and pit_macro.csv, historical vintages of CPI from the St. Louis Fed's ALFRED database. The src folder has three modules: mandate.py, engine.py and point_in_time.py."

[01:00 - 02:10] MANDATE AND OPTIMIZER
"mandate.py turns the Foundation's covenants into hard rules. The check covers total weight of 100 percent, long-only, a 3.0 percent sovereign reserve floor, the 6.50 percent return hurdle, five-day liquidity, and an 8 percent single-issuer cap.
In engine.py, build_covariance builds the covariance matrix from sleeve volatilities and correlations. optimize_constrained_mix runs a 2 percent step grid search over every long-only allocation, throws out any that break the reserve floor or miss the 6.50 percent hurdle, and keeps the one with the lowest volatility. The answer is 52 percent VT, 40 percent LQD, 8 percent IEF, no BIL: 6.50 percent return at 9.30 percent volatility."

[02:10 - 03:00] STRESS ENGINE
"run_stress_test lets the committee change estimates, costs, correlations, the estimation window and the constraints. Return haircuts, a credit spread shock, an equity-bond correlation spike and transaction costs all feed through. I added two more drivers: an estimation window of 12 to 60 months, which re-estimates volatilities and correlations from the price file, and a reserve floor from 3 to 12 percent. If volatility goes above 12.00 percent, the optimization is infeasible, or return falls below 6.50 percent, the kill rule stops the process."

[03:00 - 03:35] POINT-IN-TIME DATA
"point_in_time.py stores two dates for every observation: when the event happened and when it was published. Only data published on or before the decision date is allowed in. ALFRED shows that about 60 months of CPI were revised by seasonal-factor updates, so CPI is under quarantine."

[03:35 - 04:35] TESTS
"The suite has nine tests, and all pass.
Test 1 checks correlation symmetry and diagonal equal to 1. Tests 2 and 3 match equal weight, 5.20 percent return and 5.21 percent volatility, and the 60/40 benchmark, 6.80 percent and 10.46 percent, to four decimals. Test 4 checks the optimizer's answer. Test 5 checks the kill rule at a 100 basis point equity shock. Test 6 checks that the mandate checker passes the optimal portfolio and fails the other two. Test 7 flips the shock to confirm the kill rule turns on and off. Test 8 confirms the numbers in the memo and validation record equal engine output. Test 9 checks the new window and reserve-floor drivers."
